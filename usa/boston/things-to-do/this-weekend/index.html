This page is our standing answer to "what should I do in Boston this weekend?" The Friday-to-Sunday plan below, the recurring events, the picks by interest and the rainy-day fallback all stay useful whichever weekend you read it. 03 · Friday night

Friday night in Boston

Dinner and a cannoli in the North End

Hanover St and Salem StT Haymarket (Orange, Green)$30–60pp

The standard first-night Boston experience. Reservations are hard, so book ahead or eat early; smaller Salem and Prince Street places take walk-ins more readily.

A drink with a view in the Seaport

Lookout Rooftop, 70 Sleeper StT Courthouse (Silver)Seasonal, weekend lines

Loud and young. The Lookout has the skyline across Fort Point Channel; Fan Pier Park is free and has the same view from ground level.

Comedy or live music

Laugh Boston, 425 Summer StT World Trade Center (Silver)Shows 7:30 & 9:45pm

Friday is the best night for stand-up at Laugh Boston, and Wally's Cafe has had live jazz nightly since 1947 with no cover.

04 · Saturday

Saturday in Boston

Haymarket at 8am

Blackstone St, DowntownT Haymarket (Orange, Green)Cash

An open-air produce market on the same block since 1830. Vendors shout prices, boxes of peaches go for a few dollars. Over by dinner.

The Freedom Trail from Faneuil Hall to Copp's Hill

Faneuil Hall, 4 S Market StT Government Center (Blue, Green)~1.5 mi, 90 min

The north half of the trail: Paul Revere House, Old North Church, Copp's Hill Burying Ground with its harbor view. Ends back on Hanover Street.

Ferry to Spectacle Island

Long Wharf North, 66 Long WharfT Aquarium (Blue)~$25pp, May–Oct

A 20-minute ferry ride to a swimming beach, five miles of trails and the highest point in the harbor. Bring food; the island café is limited.

A morning at the Museum of Fine Arts

465 Huntington AveT Museum of Fine Arts (Green E)$27

Arrive at opening and choose a wing: the Americas, the Japanese temple room, or Egyptian. The Gardner is a five-minute walk away.

A Red Sox game at Fenway Park

4 Jersey St, FenwayT Kenmore (Green)From ~$20

If the Sox are home, this is the plan. Get there 90 minutes early to walk Jersey Street, closed to traffic on game days.

05 · Sunday

Sunday in Boston

SoWa Open Market

460 Harrison Ave, South EndT Tufts Medical Center (Orange)Sun 11–4, May–Oct

About 200 makers, a farmers market, food trucks, a vintage market in a former power station. Fills up by 1pm.

Harvard Square and the Harvard Art Museums

32 Quincy St, CambridgeT Harvard (Red)Museums free, Sun 10–5

Bookstores, street musicians, the Yard, and the museums, free since 2023. The Weeks Footbridge is five minutes south.

Sunset on the Esplanade

Dock by the Hatch ShellT Charles/MGH (Red)Free

Facing west over the Charles toward Cambridge. Sailboats coming in, the Citgo sign lighting up behind you.

Brunch, then the Public Garden

Between Arlington, Beacon, Charles & Boylston StT Arlington (Green)Always open

A late brunch, then the Ducklings, the Swan Boats in season, and a walk down Commonwealth Avenue Mall.

07

If it rains this weekend

Saturday, wet version: the Museum of Fine Arts at opening, then the Isabella Stewart Gardner Museum next door, with its glass-roofed courtyard. Lunch at Time Out Market, five minutes away. In the afternoon, Bates Hall at the Boston Public Library, then the covered walk through the Prudential Center.

Sunday, wet version: the Boston Public Market and Quincy Market for a late breakfast, the New England Aquarium if you have patience for lines, and an afternoon film at the Coolidge Corner Theatre or the Brattle.

The full rain plan is in things to do in Boston when it rains.

08

What's on and what's changing

The last of the North End feasts

Hanover St and North StT Haymarket (Orange)Weekend evenings, late Jul–mid Sep

Italian religious feasts run every weekend from late July. A procession, a street closed to traffic, sausage and pepper stands, a brass band. Check the schedule; dates shift year to year.

Fall foliage

Arnold Arboretum, Esplanade, Comm Ave MallPeak mid–late October

First color shows in the second half of September in the Arboretum and along the Esplanade. Peters Hill has the best skyline view.

Head of the Charles

BU Bridge to Eliot BridgeT Harvard / Central (Red)Third weekend of October, free

The world's largest two-day rowing regatta: about 11,000 rowers, free to watch from the Weeks Footbridge or the Esplanade.

End-of-season dates to know

VariousCheck before you go

Swan Boats stop mid-September; Harbor Islands ferry goes weekend-only after Columbus Day; Red Sox season ends late September; SoWa's last Sunday is late October.

09

Weekend logistics

Getting around. The T runs from about 6am until roughly 12:30am on weekends, and a ride is $2.40; a 7-day pass is $22.50. Weekend track work is common, so check the MBTA alerts on Friday. Everything in the plan above is in the central neighborhoods: Downtown to the North End is 10 minutes, to the Seaport 15, to Copley 20.

Reservations. Book Friday and Saturday dinner in the North End, South End and Seaport a week ahead in peak season. Museums don't require timed tickets most weekends, but the ICA and Gardner get crowded after 1pm.

Driving. Don't, if you can avoid it. Weekend parking downtown is expensive and the North End and Fenway are effectively closed to visitor cars on Friday nights and game days.

FAQ

Boston this weekend: common questions

What is there to do in Boston this weekend for free?

Every weekend: the Freedom Trail, the Harborwalk, the Public Garden, Haymarket on Friday and Saturday, and Harvard Square. From May through October, SoWa Open Market on Sunday. The Harvard Art Museums are free every day, and the ICA is free on Thursday evenings if you can start the weekend early.

Is the SoWa market open this weekend?

SoWa Open Market runs every Sunday from early May through the end of October, 11am to 4pm, at 460 Harrison Avenue in the South End. It closes for the season after the last Sunday in October and reopens the following May.

What is the best way to get around Boston on a weekend?

Walk and take the T. Weekend subway service runs from about 6am until roughly 12:30am, and a single ride is $2.40. Downtown, the North End, Beacon Hill, Back Bay and the Seaport are all within a 30-minute walk of each other. Avoid driving into the North End or Fenway on a game day.

Is Boston busy on weekends in the fall?

Yes. September and October are among Boston's busiest months, with students back, conventions in the Seaport and foliage visitors arriving. Book restaurants for Friday and Saturday nights, go to museums early, and expect lines at popular North End bakeries in the afternoon.

What can I do in Boston this weekend if it rains?

The Museum of Fine Arts, the ICA, the Isabella Stewart Gardner Museum, the Boston Public Library, the Boston Public Market and Quincy Market, the connected Prudential Center and Copley Place malls, and the Coolidge Corner and Brattle cinemas are all fully indoor and open on weekends.

Are the Red Sox playing at Fenway this weekend?

The Red Sox regular season runs from late March or early April through the last weekend of September, with roughly half of the weekends at home. Check the schedule on the MLB site before you plan; if there is a home game, Kenmore Square and Lansdowne Street will be crowded three hours before first pitch.
