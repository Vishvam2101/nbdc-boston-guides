Chinatown · Orange

Boston's Chinatown is small, dense and walkable in under a mile, which makes it a natural dumpling crawl: start at the gate, work through a few blocks of soup dumplings, bubble tea and egg tarts, and finish with noodles if there's room. Published as a challenge this fall with XP for every stop.

What the route covers

1

Start at the gate.

The paifang gate on Beach Street, a 1982 gift from Taipei, marks the neighborhood's entrance.

2

Soup dumplings.

Xiaolongbao done right, eaten standing over the plate so the broth doesn't go anywhere.

3

Bubble tea and egg tarts.

A sweet stop between savory ones, the way the neighborhood actually eats.

4

Noodles, if you have room.

Hand-pulled, the optional last stop for anyone still hungry.

Why does the route start at a gate?

The paifang gate on Beach Street, a 1982 gift from Taipei, marks Chinatown's main entrance and makes an obvious, easy-to-find stop one.

Can I do a Chinatown dumpling crawl before this launches?

Yes. Start at the Beach Street gate and work through soup dumplings, bubble tea and egg tarts within a few blocks; the whole loop is under a mile.
