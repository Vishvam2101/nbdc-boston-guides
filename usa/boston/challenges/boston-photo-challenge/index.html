What's on the list

1

Acorn Street.

The most photographed cobblestone lane in the country, shot from the bottom looking up.

2

The purple windows.

63–64 Beacon Street, panes turned lavender by a flaw in 1820s English glass.

3

Trinity reflected in the Hancock tower.

The cleanest reflection shot in the city, from the Boylston Street side.

A skyline shot most visitors never find, worth the walk to Cambridge.
