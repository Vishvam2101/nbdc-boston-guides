Giacomo's is cash-only with a line worth standing in; Neptune Oyster seats couples fast; Mamma Maria is the one to book a week ahead. Cheap and great: Galleria Umberto and Regina. More in the North End guide.

Cross Atlantic Avenue from Hanover Street to the lit rose trellis, then follow the water past Long Wharf and the Aquarium to the Rowes Wharf arch. About a mile round trip, busy enough to feel safe.

The Freedom Trail, Common to Old North Church

Starts at 139 Tremont StPark Street (Red/Green)About 2 miles, 2.5 hours with stopsFree to walk

Follow the red brick line through the first eleven sites and leave Charlestown for another trip. Go inside the Granary Burying Ground, the Old State House and the Old North Church. Every stop is in the Freedom Trail guide.

The Public Garden, Newbury Street and the Boston Public Library

4 Charles St · BPL, 700 Boylston StArlington or Copley (Green)Library Sat 9–5; Swan Boats mid-April to early SeptemberFree; Swan Boats about $4.50

Swan Boats and the Make Way for Ducklings statues, then eight blocks of Newbury Street, then Bates Hall and the courtyard at the library. Full neighborhood in the Back Bay guide.

Beacon Hill

Acorn St · Louisburg Square · Charles StCharles/MGH (Red) or Park StreetAlways openFree

Brick sidewalks, gas lamps and Federal townhouses. Acorn Street is the photo, Louisburg Square the grand address, Mount Vernon Street the prettiest walk between them. Route in the Beacon Hill guide.

Sunset on the Esplanade

Hatch Memorial Shell, 47 David G. Mugar WayCharles/MGH (Red)Always openFree

Cross the Arthur Fiedler footbridge from Charles Street and walk west to the Hatch Shell dock, which faces straight up the river. The best of the sunset spots in Boston.

Saturday night out

Lookout Rooftop, 70 Sleeper St · Wally's Café, 427 Mass Ave · Laugh Boston, 425 Summer St · The Beehive, 541 Tremont StCourthouse (Silver) · Massachusetts Ave (Orange) · South Station · Back Bay (Orange)Most bars close 1–2amCocktails $15–18; shows $20–40

Lookout has the skyline, the Beehive has live music and a late kitchen, Wally's is jazz since 1947 with no cover, Laugh Boston books touring comics. Red Sox home? Fenway (4 Jersey St) beats all of it. More in Boston at night and the best rooftop bars.

Tip: the T stops around 12:30am. After that, walk or take a taxi.

Friendly Toast is big and reliable, Mike & Patty's is a tiny counter with the city's best breakfast sandwich, Lincoln Tavern is the long boozy one. Full ranking in the best brunch in Boston.

Option A: Harvard Square and MIT

Harvard Yard, Massachusetts Ave · Harvard Art Museums, 32 Quincy St · MIT, 77 Massachusetts AveHarvard (Red) · Kendall/MIT (Red)Museums Sun 10–5Free

Harvard Yard through Johnston Gate, then the free Art Museums, then two stops to Kendall for the Stata Center and Killian Court's skyline view. Walk the Longfellow Bridge back in fifteen minutes. See Harvard Square.

Option B: the Seaport and the ICA

ICA, 25 Harbor Shore Dr · Row 34, 383 Congress StCourthouse (Silver Line) or South Station (Red)ICA Sun 10–5About $20

Ten minutes from South Station. The ICA's fourth-floor glass gallery over the harbor is the point; then Fort Point for oysters at Row 34 or a sandwich at Flour. Both sides in the Seaport guide.

SoWa Open Market

460 Harrison Ave, South EndTufts Medical Center (Orange), 12-minute walk, or SL4/SL5 busSundays 10am–4pm, May through OctoberFree entry

About 150 makers, a farmers' market, food trucks and a covered vintage market, fifteen minutes from Back Bay Station. Outside market season, spend the time in the South End cafés on Tremont Street.

04

Seasonal versions: foliage, winter, Marathon and summer weekends

  • Fall (late September to late October): swap Sunday for the free Arnold Arboretum (125 Arborway, Forest Hills) or Mount Auburn Cemetery (580 Mount Auburn St). Head of the Charles is the third weekend of October; book early. See Boston in the fall.
  • Winter (December to February): start the trail at 10am, skate the Frog Pond (rentals about $15), walk Beacon Hill at 4pm when the gas lamps come on, and swap Fenway for the Bruins or Celtics at TD Garden (100 Legends Way). SoWa is closed; do the MFA or Gardner. More in Boston in winter.
  • Marathon weekend (third Monday in April): hotels double and sell out months ahead. The expo runs Friday to Sunday at the Hynes, the B.A.A. 5K is Saturday, and Monday the Red Sox play at 11am. Watch from Coolidge Corner, Kenmore or the Boylston Street finish.
  • Summer (June to August): ferry to Spectacle Island from Long Wharf on Sunday morning instead of Cambridge, free Hatch Shell concerts, Sullivan's at Castle Island, the Lawn on D. Memorial Day weekend is Boston Calling. See Boston in summer.

Where to stay for a weekend in Boston

Downtown puts Friday dinner on foot. Back Bay is best for Saturday afternoon and Sunday brunch.

Downtown and the waterfront

The Bostonian, 26 North St · Omni Parker House, 60 School St · Boston Harbor Hotel, 70 Rowes WharfHaymarket · Park Street · AquariumRoughly $230–700 a night; weekend rates often lower

The Bostonian is three minutes from Hanover Street, the Parker House is on the trail, the Harbor Hotel is the splurge. Financial district hotels (the Langham, 250 Franklin St; Hyatt Centric, 54 Devonshire St) are often the best weekend deals.

Back Bay

The Lenox, 61 Exeter St · Copley Square Hotel, 47 Huntington Ave · The Newbury, 1 Newbury StCopley or Arlington (Green)Roughly $250–800 a night

Ten minutes to the North End on the Green Line, and Back Bay Station is the Amtrak stop. The Lenox is the classic, the Newbury the grand one. Every neighborhood compared in where to stay in Boston.

Budget and Cambridge

HI Boston, 19 Stuart St · citizenM North Station, 70 Causeway St · Kimpton Marlowe, 25 Edwin H. Land Blvd, CambridgeBoylston · North Station · Lechmere (Green)Hostel dorms roughly $50–90; citizenM $180–300; Marlowe $220–400

HI Boston is a clean hostel by the Common with private rooms too. citizenM is compact and next to TD Garden. Cambridge hotels near Kendall are often cheaper on weekends, two Red Line stops from Downtown.

07

Getting there, getting around and booking

  • From New York, Amtrak Acela takes about 3h40 to South Station or Back Bay Station; the Northeast Regional is an hour slower and much cheaper.
  • From Logan, the Silver Line SL1 to South Station is free; the Blue Line serves Downtown. Taxis run $25 to $40. Hotel parking is $40 to $60 a night, so leave the car at home.
  • T fares are $2.40 by CharlieCard or contactless card; a 7-day pass is $22.50. Trains run until about 12:30am. Bluebikes are $2.95 per half hour. More in getting around Boston.
  • Book ahead: the hotel, a Friday North End reservation (Mamma Maria, Carmelina's, Bricco) and Red Sox tickets. Eat at 6pm or after 9pm to halve North End waits.
  • Best weekends: late September to October and late May to June. Avoid the weekend after Labor Day, Marathon weekend, Head of the Charles and commencement weekends unless you booked early.
  • Many North End spots are cash-only; tip 18 to 20 percent. First visit? Read the first-time visitor guide and the Boston bucket list.

FAQ

A weekend in Boston: common questions

Is a weekend enough time to see Boston?

Yes, if you arrive Friday evening. Two and a half days covers the Freedom Trail, North End, Back Bay, Beacon Hill, the Esplanade, Cambridge or a museum, and three good dinners.

What is the best weekend to visit Boston?

Late September through mid-October: best weather, Head of the Charles and early foliage. Late May and June are the warm alternative. Avoid college move-in at the start of September.

Where should I stay for a weekend in Boston?

Back Bay or Downtown. Both put Saturday on foot and are one short T ride from the North End and Cambridge. Downtown weekend rates are often lower than weekdays.

How much does a weekend in Boston cost?

Excluding travel, roughly $250 to $350 per person on a hostel budget, $700 to $1,000 mid-range, and $1,500 or more high-end. A 7-day T pass is $22.50.

What should I do in Boston on a Sunday?

Brunch, then Harvard and MIT or the Seaport and the ICA. From May to October, the SoWa Open Market in the South End runs Sundays 10am to 4pm.
