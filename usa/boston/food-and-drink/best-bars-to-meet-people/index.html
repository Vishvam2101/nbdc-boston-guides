Boston bars get friendly once there is a reason to talk to a stranger: trivia, a board game, a run club, a shared table with no other seats left. Here are the bars that supply that reason, grouped by what kind of reason it is, plus how to actually use it once you're there.

All of these are on the T. Event nights change, so check a bar's calendar before you go, and weekends tend to arrive in pre-formed groups — Tuesday through Thursday is when the room is actually open to a stranger.

01 · Built-in format

Bars with a built-in social format

Trivia, board games, bowling and shuffleboard: a reason to talk to the next table that doesn't require you to invent one.

The Burren

247 Elm St, Davis Square, SomervilleDavis (Red)No cover

Irish pub with a session up front and trivia and bands in the back. Solo players join trivia teams all the time. Format: trivia and music, check the calendar. Open daily until 1am; best on weeknights.

Democracy Brewing

35 Temple Pl, Downtown CrossingDowntown Crossing (Red/Orange)No cover

Worker-owned brewery with a long communal room and a calendar of trivia, storytelling and book clubs. Open daily, check hours; best midweek.

A rare social bar downtown that's still going after 7pm.

Knight Moves Board Game Café

1402 Beacon St, BrooklineCoolidge Corner (Green C)Cover applies

Hundreds of board games, staff who teach, and open game nights that match solo players into groups. Open afternoons to late; best on open game nights.

Lucky Strike Fenway

145 Ipswich St, FenwayKenmore (Green)Cover applies

Bowling, pool, shuffleboard and darts across several floors, so you share lanes with strangers. Open daily, late; best on non-game weeknights.

Weeknights are cheaper.

Bleacher Bar

82A Lansdowne St, FenwayKenmore (Green)No cover

Built into Fenway Park with a window onto the outfield. On game day everyone yells at the same umpire. Daily, opens early on game days; best on Red Sox home games.

Come early.

Cask 'n Flagon

62 Brookline Ave, FenwayKenmore (Green)No cover

The Red Sox bar since 1969. Open daily, late; best pre-game.

Go two hours before first pitch.

A lot of these format nights — trivia, sessions, open game nights — cost nothing to walk into. See more of what's free around the city in our guide to free things to do in Boston.

02 · Communal & beer halls

Big communal bars and beer halls

Long shared tables, no small ones. You end up next to someone whether you meant to or not.

Harpoon Beer Hall

306 Northern Ave, SeaportHarbor St (Silver Line SL2)No cover

The most social room in Boston: long communal tables, no small ones, soft pretzels with beer cheese. Walk-in, $$. Daily, check hours; busiest on weekend afternoons.

Weekend afternoons are busiest.

Trillium Fort Point & the Greenway beer garden

50 Thomson Pl, Fort Point; garden on the GreenwaySouth Station (Red/Silver)No cover

The summer Greenway beer garden is the social version: picnic tables, food trucks, after-work crowd from 5pm. Walk-in, $$. Daily; beer garden May–Oct; best in summer, after 5pm.

Night Shift Brewing, Lovejoy Wharf

1 Lovejoy Wharf, West EndNorth Station (Orange/Green)No cover

Big hall with long tables, pizza and a river patio a block from the Garden. The stop before Bruins and Celtics games. Walk-in, $$. Daily, check status first; best before Garden games.

Time Out Market bar

401 Park Dr, FenwayFenway (Green D)No cover

Food hall with one long central bar and hundreds of first-come stools at shared tables. Walk-in, $$. Daily, until late; best Friday and Saturday.

Weekend nights get lively.

Harpoon and Trillium are both an easy walk from the water. Read up on the neighborhood in our Seaport guide.

03 · Neighborhood bars

Neighborhood bars where the regulars actually talk

Small rooms, a dozen stools, no way to sit through one without joining the conversation.

Delux Café

100 Chandler St, South EndBack Bay (Orange)No cover

Tiny corner bar with Elvis memorabilia and a dozen stools. You cannot be in the room without joining the conversation. Cash-friendly, $$. Evenings, until 1am, check days; good any weeknight.

Silhouette Lounge

200 Brighton Ave, AllstonHarvard Ave (Green B)No cover

Allston dive with cheap beer, free popcorn and darts. Ask to play next. Cash preferred, $. Daily, until 2am; best Thursday to Saturday.

Bukowski Tavern

50 Dalton St, Back Bay; also 1281 Cambridge St, Inman SquareHynes Convention Center (Green)No cover

Narrow bar with a hundred-bottle beer list and a wheel you spin for a random beer. Commenting on someone's drink is normal. Historically cash-only, $$. Daily, until 2am; best on weeknights.

The Druid

1357 Cambridge St, Inman Square, CambridgeCentral (Red) or bus 91/69No cover

Small Irish pub with the best Guinness around and welcoming regulars. Daily, until 1am; sessions Sunday afternoon and Wednesday.

Sessions Sunday afternoon and Wednesday.

Brendan Behan Pub

378 Centre St, Jamaica PlainJackson Square (Orange) or bus 39No cover

Cash-only Irish pub, no kitchen (bring food in), dogs welcome, jazz and session nights. Cash only, $. Daily, until 1am; good any weeknight.

A small, cash-friendly neighborhood pub is also a very good plan when the weather turns. More ideas in our rainy day guide.

04 · Student bars

Harvard Square and Central Square: student bars

Loud, cheap and full of grad students who don't mind you talking to the next table.

Charlie's Kitchen

10 Eliot St, Harvard Square, CambridgeHarvard (Red)No cover

Diner downstairs, loud cheap bar upstairs, and a beer garden full of grad students. Talking to the next table is normal. $. Daily, until 1am or later; best summer evenings.

Phoenix Landing

512 Massachusetts Ave, Central Square, CambridgeCentral (Red)No cover

Soccer bar on weekend mornings, DJ dance floor after 10pm. $. Daily, until 1am or 2am; mornings are the most sociable.

Mornings are the most sociable.

Also worth a stop: Grendel's Den (basement bar, patio), the Hong Kong (scorpion bowls) and Felipe's roof deck, all lively until 1am near Harvard station. More in the Harvard Square guide. A night there doubles easily as an actual date — a few more ideas in our Boston date ideas guide.

05 · Run clubs & meetups

Run clubs, meetups and the bars they end at

You don't have to run to make this work — the bar afterward is the actual point.

Lamplighter Brewing

284 Broadway, CambridgeKendall/MIT or Central (Red)No cover

Converted-garage brewery with Cambridge's most popular run club: a few miles along the Charles, then beer. Daily; run club weekly.

Works even if you never run.

Dorchester Brewing Company

1250 Massachusetts Ave, DorchesterAndrew (Red) or bus 10No cover

Big taproom with rooftop greenhouse, barbecue, trivia and a busy run club. Daily; run club and events weekly.

Castle Island Brewing (10 Old Colony Ave) is similar, also with a run club.

How to actually talk to people at a bar in Boston

  1. Sit at the bar, not a table. Let the bartender introduce you.
  2. Pick a night with a format. Trivia, a game or a run club. Tuesday to Thursday is best.
  3. Talk about the thing, not yourself. "What is that beer?" works. "What do you do?" does not.
  4. Go back. The third visit is when someone asks where you have been.
  5. Go early and alone. Arrive at 6pm before groups form.
  6. Know the rules. Last call is 1am most places, the T stops around 12:30, no drink discounts by law.

FAQ

Common questions

What is the best bar to meet people in Boston?

Trivia at the Burren or Democracy Brewing, game night at Bleacher Bar, or Harpoon Beer Hall for shared tables.

Which night is best for meeting people at Boston bars?

Tuesday through Thursday, when trivia, run clubs and game nights happen. Weekend crowds arrive in groups and stay in them.

Is it normal to go to a bar alone in Boston?

Yes, especially at neighborhood pubs, breweries and trivia nights. Sit at the bar, and at trivia tell the host you are solo.

Are there run clubs that meet at Boston bars?

Yes. Lamplighter, Dorchester Brewing and Castle Island host weekly run clubs that end at the bar. Check their social feeds.
