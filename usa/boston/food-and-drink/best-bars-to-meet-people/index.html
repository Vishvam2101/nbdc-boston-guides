01

Bars with a built-in social format

The Burren

247 Elm St, Davis Square, SomervilleDavis (Red)Daily, until 1amTrivia and music; check calendar

Irish pub with a session up front and trivia and bands in the back. Solo players join trivia teams all the time. See best trivia nights.

Democracy Brewing

35 Temple Pl, Downtown CrossingDowntown Crossing (Red/Orange)Daily; check hoursTrivia and community events

Worker-owned brewery with a long communal room and a calendar of trivia, storytelling and book clubs. Rare social bar downtown after 7pm.

Knight Moves Board Game Café

1402 Beacon St, BrooklineCoolidge Corner (Green C)Afternoons to lateCover charge for games

Hundreds of board games, staff who teach, and open game nights that match solo players into groups.

Lucky Strike Fenway

145 Ipswich St, FenwayKenmore (Green)Daily, lateBowling, pool, shuffleboard; lane fees

Bowling, pool, shuffleboard and darts across several floors, so you share lanes with strangers. Weeknights are cheaper.

Bleacher Bar

82A Lansdowne St, FenwayKenmore (Green)Daily; opens early on game daysBest on game night

Built into Fenway Park with a window onto the outfield. On game day everyone yells at the same umpire. Come early.

Cask 'n Flagon

62 Brookline Ave, FenwayKenmore (Green)Daily, lateBest before Red Sox games

The Red Sox bar since 1969. Go two hours before first pitch. More in our Fenway guide.

02

Big communal bars and beer halls

Harpoon Beer Hall

306 Northern Ave, SeaportHarbor St (Silver Line SL2)Daily; check hoursWalk-in; $$

The most social room in Boston: long communal tables, no small ones, soft pretzels with beer cheese. Weekend afternoons are busiest.

Trillium Fort Point and the Greenway beer garden

50 Thomson Pl, Fort Point; garden on the GreenwaySouth Station (Red/Silver)Daily; beer garden May–OctWalk-in; $$

The summer Greenway beer garden is the social version: picnic tables, food trucks, after-work crowd from 5pm. More in best breweries.

Night Shift Brewing, Lovejoy Wharf

1 Lovejoy Wharf, West EndNorth Station (Orange/Green)Daily; check status firstWalk-in; $$

Big hall with long tables, pizza and a river patio a block from the Garden. The stop before Bruins and Celtics games.

Time Out Market bar

401 Park Dr, FenwayFenway (Green D)Daily, until lateWalk-in; $$

Food hall with one long central bar and hundreds of first-come stools at shared tables. Weekend nights get lively.

Neighborhood bars where the regulars actually talk

Delux Café

100 Chandler St, South EndBack Bay (Orange)Evenings, until 1am; check daysCash-friendly; $$

Tiny corner bar with Elvis memorabilia and a dozen stools. You cannot be in the room without joining the conversation.

Silhouette Lounge

200 Brighton Ave, AllstonHarvard Ave (Green B)Daily, until 2amCash preferred; $

Allston dive with cheap beer, free popcorn and darts. Ask to play next. More in our dive bars guide.

Bukowski Tavern

50 Dalton St, Back Bay; also 1281 Cambridge St, Inman SquareHynes Convention Center (Green)Daily, until 2amHistorically cash-only; $$

Narrow bar with a hundred-bottle beer list and a wheel you spin for a random beer. Commenting on someone's drink is normal.

The Druid

1357 Cambridge St, Inman Square, CambridgeCentral (Red) or bus 91/69Daily, until 1am$$

Small Irish pub with the best Guinness around and welcoming regulars. Sessions Sunday afternoon and Wednesday. More in best Irish pubs.

Brendan Behan Pub

378 Centre St, Jamaica PlainJackson Square (Orange) or bus 39Daily, until 1amCash only; $

Cash-only Irish pub, no kitchen (bring food in), dogs welcome, jazz and session nights. More in our Jamaica Plain guide.

Soccer bar on weekend mornings, DJ dance floor after 10pm. Mornings are the most sociable. More in our Central Square guide.

05

Run clubs, meetups and the bars they end at

Lamplighter Brewing

284 Broadway, CambridgeKendall/MIT or Central (Red)Daily; run club weekly$$

Converted-garage brewery with Cambridge's most popular run club: a few miles along the Charles, then beer. Works even if you never run.

Dorchester Brewing Company

1250 Massachusetts Ave, DorchesterAndrew (Red) or bus 10Daily; run club and events weekly$$

Big taproom with rooftop greenhouse, barbecue, trivia and a busy run club. Castle Island Brewing (10 Old Colony Ave) is similar.

06

How to actually talk to people at a bar in Boston

  • Sit at the bar, not a table. Let the bartender introduce you.
  • Pick a night with a format. Trivia, a game or a run club. Tuesday to Thursday is best.
  • Talk about the thing, not yourself. "What is that beer?" works. "What do you do?" does not.
  • Go back. The third visit is when someone asks where you have been.
  • Go early and alone. Arrive at 6pm before groups form. See things to do alone.
  • Know the rules. Last call is 1am most places, the T stops around 12:30, no drink discounts by law.

FAQ

Common questions

What is the best bar to meet people in Boston?

Trivia at the Burren or Democracy Brewing, game night at Bleacher Bar, or Harpoon Beer Hall for shared tables.

Which night is best for meeting people at Boston bars?

Tuesday through Thursday, when trivia, run clubs and game nights happen. Weekend crowds arrive in groups and stay in them.

Is it normal to go to a bar alone in Boston?

Yes, especially at neighborhood pubs, breweries and trivia nights. Sit at the bar, and at trivia tell the host you are solo.

Are there run clubs that meet at Boston bars?

Yes. Lamplighter, Dorchester Brewing and Castle Island host weekly run clubs that end at the bar. Check their social feeds.
