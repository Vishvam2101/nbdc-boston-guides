Boston bars get friendly once there is a reason to talk to a stranger: trivia, a board game, a run club, a shared table with no other seats left. Here are the bars that supply that reason, grouped by what kind of reason it is, plus how to actually use it once you're there.

All of these are on the T. Event nights change, so check a bar's calendar before you go, and weekends tend to arrive in pre-formed groups — Tuesday through Thursday is when the room is actually open to a stranger.

How to actually talk to people at a bar in Boston

  1. 1Sit at the bar, not a table. Let the bartender introduce you.
  2. 2Pick a night with a format. Trivia, a game or a run club. Tuesday to Thursday is best.
  3. 3Talk about the thing, not yourself. "What is that beer?" works. "What do you do?" does not.
  4. 4Go back. The third visit is when someone asks where you have been.
  5. 5Go early and alone. Arrive at 6pm before groups form.
  6. 6Know the rules. Last call is 1am most places, the T stops around 12:30, no drink discounts by law.

FAQ

Common questions

What is the best bar to meet people in Boston?+
Trivia at the Burren or Democracy Brewing, game night at Bleacher Bar, or Harpoon Beer Hall for shared tables.
Which night is best for meeting people at Boston bars?+
Tuesday through Thursday, when trivia, run clubs and game nights happen. Weekend crowds arrive in groups and stay in them.
Is it normal to go to a bar alone in Boston?+
Yes, especially at neighborhood pubs, breweries and trivia nights. Sit at the bar, and at trivia tell the host you are solo.
Are there run clubs that meet at Boston bars?+
Yes. Lamplighter, Dorchester Brewing and Castle Island host weekly run clubs that end at the bar. Check their social feeds.
